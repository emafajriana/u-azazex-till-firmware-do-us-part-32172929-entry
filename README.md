Entry for the [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929) bounty on make.fun: one wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding, funny and heartfelt in a single sentence under 200 characters.

## The vow

> I promise to love you through every firmware update, even the ones that brick me, and to keep your name in my cache long after my battery dies.

A machine's "until death do us part": love through every update — even the ones that brick it — and the beloved's name kept in cache long after the battery dies.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the vow to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, then verifies the bytes on disk are exactly the vow's UTF-8 encoding, 1–200 characters, one sentence.
